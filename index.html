What it is

Plinko Chaos is a free idle game. You start with a nearly empty board and buy pins one at a time, unlock wider rows, and watch a growing swarm of balls ricochet down and pile coins into the pots below. Leave it running and it keeps paying — come back and spend everything on the next upgrade.

Features

A board you actually build

Every pin is bought by hand. Rows widen as you unlock them, so the field you play on is one you paid for, peg by peg.

Ten ball colours, ten powers

White through Gold, each worth more than the last, and each with its own status: Bouncy, Electric, Magnet, Lucky, Plasma, Frost, Split, Prism, Explosive and Midas.

Prestige into Builds

Cash a run in for Stars and spend them on permanent Builds — deeper boards, golden pegs, a jackpot bucket, quantum pegs, even up to four boards running side by side.

Earns while you're away

The board keeps working offline and greets you with the take when you return. Milestones hand out gems for the long haul.

How it plays

  • Tap to drop — or let each unlocked colour rain on its own timer.
  • Every peg pays. Bounces charge a ball; the pot it lands in multiplies the take.
  • Spend it all. Pins, rows, ball power, status chances — then prestige and do it bigger.

Free, with optional purchases

The whole game is playable for free. It shows ads, and optional purchases can remove them, add gems, or hand you a starter boost. Nothing in the game is real-money gambling: coins, gems and Stars are in-game items with no cash value, and nothing can be cashed out. See the terms for the details.
