Plinko Chaos Support Privacy Terms

Privacy Policy

Plinko Chaos, by Gies Games. Effective date: 26 July 2026. Replaces the version dated 8 July 2026.

Plinko Chaos is a free idle game for iOS and Android. This policy explains what data the app handles and why. The short version: there are no accounts, your game progress stays on your device, and the only third parties involved are the ones that power advertising, purchases and app updates.

Who we are

The app is published by Gies Games, an independent developer. For any privacy question, including a request about your data, write to gieske166@gmail.com.

Data stored on your device

Your game progress — coins, gems, Stars, upgrades, builds, milestones and settings — is saved locally on your device. It is not uploaded to us, and we operate no game server and no account system. The app also keeps a short local log of technical errors (the last few, with the error message and stack trace) so a crash can be diagnosed later; that log never leaves your device. Deleting the app deletes both.

Advertising (Google AdMob)

The app shows ads through Google AdMob: banners on menu screens, an occasional interstitial, and optional rewarded ads that only play when you choose to watch one. To serve, measure and protect those ads against fraud, Google may process device information such as advertising identifiers, IP address, device type and ad interaction data.

In the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom and Switzerland, a Google-certified consent dialog is shown before ads are loaded, and your choice there governs whether the ads you see are personalised. Where that dialog does not apply, ads may be personalised in line with Google's own policies and your device settings. You can limit this at any time from your device's advertising settings (iOS: Settings › Privacy & Security › Tracking and Apple Advertising; Android: Settings › Privacy › Ads).

Details: Google Privacy Policy, How Google uses data from ads.

Purchases (RevenueCat and the app stores)

In-app purchases are processed entirely by Apple or Google. To recognise your purchases and restore them after a reinstall, the app uses RevenueCat, which receives an anonymous app-generated identifier and purchase receipt data. RevenueCat does not receive your name, email address or payment details — those stay with Apple or Google, and we never see them. See the RevenueCat Privacy Policy.

App updates (Expo)

On launch the app may check for updated game code through Expo's update service (EAS Update). That request carries standard connection data such as your IP address, device platform and the installed app version, used only to deliver the correct update. See the Expo Privacy Policy.

What we do not do

  • No accounts, no registration, no login.
  • We do not collect your name, email address, phone number or location.
  • No separate analytics or attribution SDK is built into the app.
  • We do not sell your data, and we do not track you across other apps or websites.

Children

Plinko Chaos is not directed to children under 13 (or the equivalent minimum age in your country), and we do not knowingly collect personal data from them. If you believe a child has provided personal data through the app, contact us and we will act on it.

Your choices and rights

  • Remove ads — the No Ads purchase removes banner and interstitial ads.
  • Reset or limit ad tracking — from your device's advertising settings, at any time.
  • Reset ad consent — where the consent dialog applies, uninstalling and reinstalling the app clears the stored choice and asks again. You can also write to us.
  • Delete everything — deleting the app removes all data it stored on your device.

Depending on where you live, you may have rights to access, correct, delete or restrict the processing of personal data, or to object to it. Because we hold no personal data ourselves, such requests are best addressed to Google (advertising) or Apple/Google (purchases); we will help you direct them if you write to us.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the updated version is published at this address with a new effective date. Material changes will also be noted in the app's store listing.
