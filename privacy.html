Privacy Policy

Plinko Chaos, by GiesGames. Effective date: July 8, 2026.

Plinko Chaos is a free idle game. This policy explains what data the app handles and why. The short version: there are no accounts, your game progress stays on your device, and the only third parties involved are the ones that power advertising and purchases.

Data stored on your device

Your game progress (coins, upgrades, milestones, settings) is saved locally on your device. It is not uploaded to us. Deleting the app deletes this data. The app also keeps a small local log of technical errors to help fix bugs; this log never leaves your device.

Advertising (Google AdMob)

The app shows ads through Google AdMob, including optional rewarded ads that you choose to watch. AdMob may process device information such as advertising identifiers, IP address, and ad interaction data to serve and measure ads and to prevent fraud. The app requests non-personalized ads. In the European Economic Area and the United Kingdom, a consent dialog is shown before any ads are loaded, and you can decline. For details, see the Google Privacy Policy and How Google uses data from ads.

Purchases (RevenueCat and the app stores)

In-app purchases are processed by Apple or Google. To recognize your purchases and restore them across reinstalls, the app uses RevenueCat, which receives an anonymous app-generated identifier and purchase receipt data. RevenueCat does not receive your name, email address, or payment details; those stay with Apple or Google. See the RevenueCat Privacy Policy.

App updates (Expo)

On launch the app may check for updated game code via Expo's update service (EAS Update). This request involves standard connection data such as your IP address and the app version, used only to deliver the correct update.

What we do not do

No accounts, no registration, no collection of names or email addresses, no selling of data, no tracking across other apps or websites by us. We do not knowingly collect personal data from children; the app requests non-personalized ads for everyone.

Your choices

You can remove all ads with the No Ads purchase. You can withdraw or change ad consent in your device or via the consent dialog where applicable. You can reset advertising identifiers in your device settings at any time.
