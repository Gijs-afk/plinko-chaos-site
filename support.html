Plinko Chaos Support Privacy Terms

Support

Plinko Chaos is made by one developer, and every message is read personally. Bugs, lost progress, purchase trouble, or just an idea for the board — all welcome.

Email support

Typical reply within a few days. To get a fix rather than a follow-up question, please include:

  • Your device and OS version (e.g. iPhone 13, iOS 18.2)
  • The app version and build, shown at the bottom of the in-game Store tab
  • What you did, what happened, and what you expected
  • A screenshot or screen recording if anything looks wrong on screen

Common questions

I bought an upgrade and nothing changed.

Some tracks have a ceiling — a status spawn chance stops at 50%, and Ball Count stops once you have hit the board's live-ball limit. At the ceiling the button reads MAX and no longer takes coins. If you see a purchase go through with no visible change and no MAX label, that is a bug: mail us the upgrade name and your level and it will be fixed.

My progress is gone.

Progress is saved locally on your device, not to an account or the cloud. Deleting the app, or restoring a device from a backup that did not include app data, removes it. There is no way for us to recover a save we never received — but if progress vanished while the app stayed installed, that is a bug worth reporting, so please write in.

I paid, but I still see ads / did not get my items.

Open the in-game Store tab and use Restore Purchases — that re-reads your entitlements from the App Store or Google Play. If it still does not appear, email us with the store receipt (Apple emails one; Google shows the order in Play) and it will be sorted out.

How do refunds work?

Purchases are handled entirely by Apple and Google — we never see your payment details and we cannot issue refunds ourselves. Request one through Apple's Report a Problem or Google Play's refund page. If a purchase failed because of a bug in the game, write to us as well so the bug gets fixed.

A rewarded ad did not give me the reward.

The reward only lands if the ad plays through to the end. Ads that fail to load, or that get closed early, do not pay out — the offer stays available, so you can try again in a moment. Weak connections are the usual cause.

Can I turn off ads?

Yes — the No Ads purchase in the Store tab removes banner and interstitial ads permanently. Optional rewarded ads (the ones you tap to watch for a bonus) stay available by choice.

What are offline earnings?

The board keeps paying while the app is closed, at a share of its live rate and up to a capped number of hours. You collect the total on your next launch. The Coin Fountain build and the Time Capsule and Night Shift power-ups raise the share and the cap.

How do I delete my data?

Deleting the app removes everything it stored, since the save and the local error log live only on your device. See the privacy policy for what the ad and purchase providers hold and how to reach them.

Is this gambling?

No. There is no wagering of real money and nothing can be cashed out. Coins, gems and Stars are in-game items with no monetary value.
